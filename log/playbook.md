# Playbook: patterns that work (max 15 bullets, updated by the daily research)

- Visible escalation/progression ("1 to 100", small -> bigger -> biggest) holds viewers to the end (Domino Vexa 21M, 2026-11-12).
- Comment questions in title/end ("which one is funniest?", "which grade is correct") drive comments (Sir Potato, OXXY, 2026-11-12).
- Hook scorer: short hooks (~15 words) with a number and the keyword first score 70-80+; long hooks score under 50 (our tests, 2026-11-11/12).
- What If hooks must be <=12 words with the consequence and a number in the first line ("cut your power for up to two years") to score 75+; question-only openers score ~40 (hook tests, 2026-11-13).
- "What If You could see/know hidden social info" (who has a crush on you, everyone's secret, your luck) is the strongest What If You cluster: 1.9M / 594K / 790K on @JeremyBearimyTheory (outlierkit, 2026-11-15).
- Hook scorer: "What if you could see who has a crush on you?" scores ~38 (no number, no stakes); front-loading "for ten seconds" + "secret" lifts it to 81 (hook tests, 2026-11-15).
- A What If You question that other channels turn into a multi-part series ("how long everyone has left", Part 3/4 seen 2026-11-15) signals demand; view counts for it could not be verified. Hook scorer: adding "secret" + "for ten minutes" up front lifts a plain question from 55 to 81 (2026-11-15).
- Queue questions (stop time, future wealth, woke up 20K years ago) each have many uploads from different channels (9+ for future wealth in ~3 weeks), views not verifiable (2026-11-16/17). Hook scorer: a stake lifts each to ~78-81 ("for ten seconds" 77.8; "secret money" + "for ten minutes" 81.4 vs 55; "no phone" 54.8 -> 80.8).
- Classic superpowers need a stake word to score: "What if you could fly for one day?" 37.8 vs "...for 24 hours, no plane?" 80.8 (hook tests, 2026-11-18); "fly, but only for 60 seconds" 81.4 vs "for just one minute" 37.8 (2026-11-26): a digit beats "one minute". Tiny channels (70-250 subs) got 27K-167K with plain one-power questions (invisible 1.28M, x-ray 27K, odds of everything 27K, folded paper 167K at 20 s; vidIQ 2026-11-26).
