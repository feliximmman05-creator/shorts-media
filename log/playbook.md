# Playbook: patterns that work (max 15 bullets, updated by the daily research)

- Superlative spectacle titles ("World's Biggest X", "Max Speed") on fresh stunts pull huge vph (Red Bull catapult 30M/3 days, vidIQ 2026-11-12).
- Visible escalation/progression ("1 to 100", small -> bigger -> biggest) holds viewers to the end (Domino Vexa 21M, 2026-11-12).
- Ride the week's mega-trend with crossovers (GTA 6 x footballers, 14M, 2026-11-12).
- Comment questions in title/end ("which one is funniest?", "which grade is correct") drive comments (Sir Potato, OXXY, 2026-11-12).
- Animated game-style characters (Minecraft/Roblox/Sonic) dominate Shorts vph; cartoon style fits (2026-11-12).
- Hook scorer: short hooks (~15 words) with a number and the keyword first score 70-80+; long hooks score under 50 (our tests, 2026-11-11/12).
- What If hooks must be <=12 words with the consequence and a number in the first line ("cut your power for up to two years") to score 75+; question-only openers score ~40 (hook tests, 2026-11-13).
- Space-weather news (big sunspot facing Earth, auroras far south) spawns many "solar storm" Shorts/lives the same week; good hook for Earth/space What Ifs (web, 2026-11-13).
- "You are there watching it fall" space scenarios pull 1M+ (What If channel, Space Station fall, 1.5M in 13 days, outlierkit 2026-11-13).
